3. Guide for the group meeting

AGC target
Broad signals above the precursor persist across AGC 100, 300 and 1000. A substantial reduction is not apparent.

Spray voltage
The highest FMN precursor maximum in these averaged data is at 3.5 kV, but high-mass features remain at every voltage.

LED photoactivation
Major LED ON products include m/z 243, 257, 286 and 359. The above-precursor features are also visible LED OFF.

Important limitations
LED ON used isolation centre 457.00 versus LED OFF 457.11. The data are averaged spectra, so they cannot diagnose scan-level intermittency.

Next checks: solvent blank, individual scans and a matched OFF → ON → OFF sequence with a fixed isolation centre.
